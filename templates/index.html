TruthLens

Multilingual News Analysis, Propaganda Detection & Media Literacy Platform

TruthLens is an AI-powered media literacy application engineered to help citizens, students, and researchers critically evaluate online news, viral forwards, and social media claims. Instead of issuing arbitrary "fake" or "real" labels, TruthLens breaks down each factual statement, verifies exact supporting passages, detects persuasive manipulation, and cross-references empirical evidence across English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, and Kannada.

Why TruthLens Was Built

Solving 4 Critical Media Literacy Problems

Addressing the growing wave of unverified claims, emotional manipulation, and language barriers.

1. Viral Misinformation

Sensational rumors and unsubstantiated claims spread rapidly across WhatsApp and social platforms without evidence checks.

TruthLens Fix: Extracts checkable empirical claims and connects them to exact article passages.

2. Emotional Manipulation

Phrases like "Urgent alert!" or "Share before it's deleted" manipulate fear and urgency to bypass readers' critical faculties.

TruthLens Fix: Identifies persuasive language devices with quotes and explains likely reader impact.

3. Language Barriers

Most fact-checking tools serve only English, leaving readers of regional Indian languages vulnerable to local misdirection.

TruthLens Fix: Native processing for English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, and Kannada.

4. Unverifiable Claims

Readers often cannot distinguish between claims that are demonstrably false versus claims that simply lack sufficient evidence.

TruthLens Fix: Never labels a claim false without proof; transparently marks unverified statements.

End-to-End Technical Pipeline

How TruthLens Analyzes Content

1

Ingestion & Security Sanitization

Content is parsed via URL extraction or pasted text. Built-in SSRF protections block private IP ranges, while clutter and advertisements are stripped cleanly.

2

Claim Extraction & Quotation Grounding

Individual factual claims are isolated and strictly linked to verbatim passages in the source text. Non-existent quotes and fabricated claims are rejected by validation filters.

3

Evidence Assessment & Transparent Reporting

Claims receive one of 5 plain-English verdicts with documented limitations. Stylistic persuasive devices and missing context are reported in a 3-tab interactive dashboard.

Academic Defense & Viva Guide

Questions Frequently Asked in Capstone Evaluations

Quick conceptual answers to explain your project architecture confidently during college viva.

Q1: Why not a simple "Fake" vs "Real" score?

Binary labels are fundamentally flawed. Real news can contain sensational language, while false stories often incorporate factual dates. TruthLens breaks down each claim individually so readers understand exact nuances.

Q2: How does TruthLens prevent AI hallucination?

Every extracted claim is programmatically checked against the source text. If a quote or sentence does not exist in the submitted article, validation filters discard it before rendering.

Q3: How does the offline mode function?

When no API key is provided, TruthLens executes deterministic local rule-based NLP algorithms to extract genuine sentences and flag manipulative vocabulary completely offline on port 8765.
